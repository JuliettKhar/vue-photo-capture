Photos, video, cropping, QR scanning and hardware controls — a composable and a drop-in component.

Live Demo

📸 Photos & full-res stills

Capture the current frame as a Blob, or full sensor resolution via ImageCapture with a canvas fallback.

🎥 Video recording

Record clips with MediaRecorder — optional audio, pause/resume.

✂️ Crop, resize & EXIF

Crop and downscale on capture; editImage() applies EXIF orientation to any blob.

🔄 Camera switching

List devices, switch by id, or flip front/back. Reactive resolution and mirror style.

🔦 Hardware controls

Torch, zoom, focus, exposure and white balance — all capability-guarded.

🔍 QR / barcode scanning

Native BarcodeDetector, with a documented ponyfill path for Safari/Firefox.

🧩 Drop-in component

gives you a ready-made UI with slots, events and exposed methods.

♻️ Real cleanup

Tracks stop on unmount, reactive state, SSR-safe support flags, shipped TypeScript types.
